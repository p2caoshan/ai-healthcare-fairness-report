Cohorts and definitions

That join yields 32,751 pairs. The modeling table has 30,454 rows and 44 columns; the notebooks do not print why those two counts differ. Labs are the nearest prior result within 24 hours. Cutoffs in the code. Bias is SaO2 minus SpO2. Negative means the pulse oximeter reads higher than the artery. Hypoxemia is SaO2 under 88. The device “detects” it only if SpO2 is also under 88. A false negative is arterial hypoxemia with SpO2 still at 88 or above. A separate disparate-impact check treats SpO2 at or above 92 as the favorable “safe” reading. That is not the same rule as the research definition SaO2 < 88 and SpO2 ≥ 92, which these notebooks do not compute.

Causal analysis

No new model was fit here. The credentialed rows are not in this site. What follows is a reading of the printed rates, plus the assumptions that would turn a skin-group table into a claim about the probe.

The Dark-minus-Light gaps, −1.058 points on MIMIC and −0.630 on BOLD, are differences in the mean of SaO2 − SpO2. They say that, inside these paired rows, the recorded Dark group sits further toward over-reading. A device question asks something else: how SpO2 would move if skin pigment changed and arterial saturation, hemoglobin, and perfusion stayed put. Those questions meet when recorded race behaves like an intervention on pigment. Race in these files is a label, not an assignment. The Dark and Light rows also differ in illness, in labs, and in having had an arterial gas within five minutes of the pulse-ox reading.

Why the cohort itself is a conditioned collider

MIMIC keeps a row when an arterial gas and a charted SpO2 fall within five minutes. BOLD is already a blood-gas cohort. Drawing that gas is a decision. Severity pushes it. So does a pulse-ox reading that looks worse than the patient, and so do care patterns tied to recorded race. Every rate on this page is inside that selected set.

The gold node is a collider. Conditioning on it opens a path between race and severity that belongs to the sampling scheme. The 32,751 MIMIC pairs, and the 30,454 modeling rows, are that node. Saturations that never received a blood gas are unobserved, so this page cannot reweight them.

What would isolate the device path

For the arrow from pigment to SpO2, holding the physiology the probe claims to report: compare groups inside slices of SaO2, and hold hemoglobin and the dyshemoglobins when the question is pigment at a fixed blood color. Leave SpO2 on the outcome side. Leave the hidden-hypoxemia label out of the adjustment set, because it is computed from both readings. Leave pO2 out of a measurement-error model. It is drawn with SaO2 and moves with it. Race would still bundle pigment with perfusion, device, and hospital. This site cannot compute that stratified contrast. Until it exists, −1.06 and −0.63 remain descriptive gaps.

What the SaO2 model is conditioning on

Arterial pO2 is the most-used split on both cohorts, weight 291 on MIMIC and 237 on BOLD. pO2 and SaO2 are both effects of gas exchange, measured on the same draw. A fit that leans on pO2 reconstructs saturation from the blood gas. That is why BOLD can reach an R² of 0.89, and why the gain is a weak claim about a finger probe.

What was not estimated. Counterfactual fairness would ask whether the alert is unchanged under an intervention on pigment, with severity held. Path-specific fairness would allow paths that run through true illness and block the path that runs through the probe. BoundedGroupLoss is a regression penalty across groups. Calibration by group was plotted in the notebooks and not printed as a slope, so this page does not score it.

What the two cohorts support

Shared. Dark-group SpO2 over-reads relative to Light, as a difference in means inside the paired sample. A parity check at 92 still passes, and still sits above the arterial safe rate by 0.061 on MIMIC and 0.020 on BOLD. Linear regression is a poor hypoxemia alarm. The section 2 XGBoost raises Dark-group detection sharply and widens the recall spread: 0.045 to 0.130 on MIMIC, with Medium at the floor, and a Light–Dark gap from 0.007 to 0.060 on BOLD. pO2 dominates the fit. Group-loss search does not make the alarm fairer, and it does not target the optical path.
Not shared. MIMIC’s device error and hypoxemia rate are much higher. Dark versus Medium is significant only on BOLD. Medium is the weak detection group on MIMIC only. BOLD’s strong detector and BOLD’s Fairlearn refit are different trainings, and only one of them works. The Fairlearn gap of 0.0284 on MIMIC is that second training, not the 0.130 spread of the detector.

Appendix. Salk vaccine field trial

Design only. No records.

Handing the 1954 polio vaccine to a large group of children and counting cases the next year would not show that the vaccine worked. There is no control series, and nothing separates the vaccine from the other reasons polio rates move. Consistency, positivity, and ignorability are all missing. It is also the wrong first exposure for children when the evidence is still from the laboratory.

Vaccinating only the children whose parents consent is not randomization. Consent tracks exposure, schooling, and trust in medicine, and those same factors change polio risk. Ignorability fails.

Ask permission to be in the study. Then randomize the children who are in it. That order is what makes ignorability plausible. The causal section applies the same three conditions to the oximetry cohorts, where no randomization plays that role.

Limits

Race stands in for melanin, and the two maps differ. The Welch gaps are differences in means, with the back doors in the causal graph left open. The paired sample conditions on a blood gas having been drawn, which is a collider of severity, the displayed SpO2, and care patterns. One split, no intervals on the fairness metrics, and thin event counts for Medium and for BOLD’s smaller groups. Those thin cells are also where positivity fails for a finer group contrast. Median fill invents a typical lab for patients who were never measured. The fairness penalty optimizes a regression loss and is scored as a classifier. On BOLD, the model that detects and the model that is constrained are different trainings. The section 2 recall spreads are arithmetic on rounded rates. Group calibration was plotted and not printed as a slope. Nothing on this page is a reason, by itself, to change a monitor or a treatment threshold.
